M.S. student in Electronic Engineering
Sogang University, Seoul, South Korea

I'm an M.S. student at Sogang University, advised by Prof. Suk-Ju Kang in the VIDS Lab. I work on efficient inference for foundation models in computer vision: making them lighter and faster to run while keeping their performance.

My research covers post-training quantization, KV cache compression, feature caching, and long-term memory for large vision models.
